Join us

We are building a diverse, supportive and interdisciplinary team working where living systems meet medical science. Our current projects focus on computational modeling and data analysis, carried out in close collaboration with experimental groups.

If you enjoy using physics to answer fundamental biological questions and to improve human health, we would like to hear from you.

IIT Kharagpur students

Undergraduate and postgraduate students currently at IIT Kharagpur are welcome to email us directly about research projects.

PhD applicants

Apply through the IIT Kharagpur PhD program, choosing the School of Medical Science and Technology.

Postdoctoral fellows

Consider the National Post-Doctoral Fellowship or the Institute Post-Doctoral Fellowship. We are happy to support your application; send your CV and a short statement of research interests.
